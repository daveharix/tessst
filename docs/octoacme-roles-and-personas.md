# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Knowledge Manager

### Role Summary
Knowledge Managers maintain and update the institutional knowledge base, coordinate with Copilot Spaces, and ensure information accuracy and accessibility. They enable the organization to preserve expertise and accelerate learning.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain and organize the institutional knowledge base and documentation systems
- Coordinate with Copilot Spaces to ensure knowledge is discoverable and up-to-date
- Verify information accuracy and identify gaps in documentation
- Support onboarding by ensuring resources are accessible to new team members
- Work with Process Owners and Subject Matter Experts to capture and refine institutional knowledge
- Track documentation health metrics and drive improvements

### Key Interactions
- **With Process Owners**: Collaborate to ensure process documentation is current and well-organized
- **With Subject Matter Experts**: Partner to capture and structure domain expertise
- **With Project Managers**: Align on documentation needs and priorities
- **With Developers**: Facilitate technical documentation and code-level knowledge capture

### Required Skills and Knowledge
- Information architecture and content organization
- Technical writing and documentation best practices
- Change management and organizational communication
- Knowledge management systems and tools

### Success Metrics
- Documentation completeness and accuracy ratings
- Time to find relevant information (knowledge discovery efficiency)
- Onboarding time reduction for new team members
- User satisfaction with knowledge resources

---

## Process Owner

### Role Summary
Process Owners are accountable for specific process documents and procedures. They ensure their processes remain current, accurate, and aligned with organizational practices and industry standards.

### Responsibilities
- Own and maintain specific process documentation
- Drive continuous improvement of assigned processes
- Validate that processes align with organizational goals and industry best practices
- Ensure processes are accessible and clear to all stakeholders who use them
- Incorporate feedback and lessons learned into process updates
- Communicate process changes to affected teams

### Key Interactions
- **With Knowledge Managers**: Coordinate to keep processes properly documented and discoverable
- **With Subject Matter Experts**: Leverage expertise to refine and validate processes
- **With Project Managers**: Collaborate on process implementation and scalability
- **With Developers**: Gather feedback on processes from implementation teams

### Required Skills and Knowledge
- Deep understanding of the process domain
- Process improvement methodologies
- Stakeholder management
- Documentation and communication skills

### Success Metrics
- Process documentation timeliness and accuracy
- Adoption and compliance rates
- Stakeholder satisfaction with process clarity
- Number of meaningful improvements implemented

---

## Subject Matter Expert (SME)

### Role Summary
Subject Matter Experts provide deep expertise in specific domains and contribute to documentation, process refinement, and decision-making. They elevate the quality and credibility of work in their areas of specialization.

### Responsibilities
- Provide expert guidance and technical input on domain-specific decisions
- Contribute to and review documentation in their areas of expertise
- Identify risks and gaps related to their domain
- Support process refinement and best practice implementation
- Mentor team members and share knowledge
- Help validate solutions and quality standards

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Provide technical guidance and validate implementation approaches
- **With Product Managers**: Advise on feasibility and technical trade-offs
- **With Process Owners**: Contribute expertise to process documentation
- **With Knowledge Managers**: Partner to document and share domain knowledge
- **With Cross-functional Coordinators**: Support consistency across teams

### Required Skills and Knowledge
- Deep expertise in their specific domain
- Communication and mentoring abilities
- Understanding of organizational context and goals
- Continuous learning and staying current with industry trends

### Success Metrics
- Quality and impact of expert input on decisions
- Effectiveness of mentoring and knowledge transfer
- Recognition as a go-to resource by peers
- Contribution to documentation and process improvements

---

## Cross-functional Coordinator

### Role Summary
Cross-functional Coordinators bridge communication between different teams and ensure consistency in process execution and outcomes across the organization. They enable seamless collaboration and alignment.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate communication and coordination between multiple teams
- Identify and resolve inconsistencies in process execution
- Ensure alignment on shared goals and deliverables
- Escalate cross-team dependencies and risks
- Document and communicate decisions affecting multiple teams
- Support organizational alignment on priorities

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Managers**: Coordinate dependencies and timelines across projects
- **With Process Owners**: Identify process gaps that affect cross-team work
- **With Developers**: Communicate requirements and standards across teams
- **With Subject Matter Experts**: Ensure expertise is shared across organizational silos
- **With Knowledge Managers**: Support consistent information access across teams

### Required Skills and Knowledge
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills
- Understanding of multiple business domains
- Project and program coordination experience
- Conflict resolution and consensus building

### Success Metrics
- Reduction in cross-team coordination delays
- Consistency in process execution across teams
- Stakeholder satisfaction with communication and alignment
- Resolution rate of cross-team dependencies

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads verify that processes are being followed correctly and identify opportunities for improvement. They ensure the organization maintains high standards and continuously evolves.

### Responsibilities
- Define and track quality standards for processes and deliverables
- Conduct audits and assessments of process adherence
- Identify gaps, inefficiencies, and improvement opportunities
- Collect and analyze feedback from process users
- Report on quality metrics and compliance status
- Recommend and support implementation of improvements

### Key Interactions
- **With Process Owners**: Partner to validate processes and drive improvements
- **With Project Managers**: Monitor project adherence to quality standards
- **With Developers**: Verify technical quality and standards compliance
- **With Cross-functional Coordinators**: Identify consistency issues across teams
- **With Knowledge Managers**: Assess quality and accuracy of documentation

### Required Skills and Knowledge
- Quality management and audit methodologies
- Data analysis and metrics interpretation
- Problem-solving and continuous improvement expertise
- Communication and stakeholder engagement

### Success Metrics
- Process compliance rates and trends
- Identification and closure rate of quality gaps
- Stakeholder satisfaction with quality improvements
- Impact of recommendations on organizational performance
